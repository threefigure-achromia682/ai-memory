# 🧠 ai-memory - Never Lose Track of Your AI Conversations

## 🎯 What is ai-memory?

ai-memory is a simple, powerful tool that helps your AI coding assistants remember important information between sessions. Think of it as a smart notebook for your AI tools—it stores conversations, decisions, and context so you can switch between different AI agents without losing momentum. It is designed for people who use AI to write code, but you do not need to be a programmer to benefit from it.

Instead of starting from scratch every time you open a new AI session, ai-memory saves the important stuff automatically. You can pause a project today, come back next week, and your AI assistant will know exactly where you left off. It is like giving your AI a reliable memory.

## 📦 What Can You Do With ai-memory?

- ✅ **Save conversations** – Keep a history of all your AI chats in one place.
- ✅ **Switch AI vendors easily** – Move between different AI coding tools without losing context.
- ✅ **Organize by project** – Group related chats and notes together for easy reference.
- ✅ **Search past sessions** – Find that one code fix or idea you discussed days ago.
- ✅ **Export your data** – Keep a backup or share context with teammates.
- ✅ **Works with any AI agent** – No lock-in, compatible with popular coding assistants.

Yes. All your sessions are stored locally on your computer. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to export it yourself.

### Can I use ai-memory on multiple computers?

Yes. You can export your data on one computer and import it on another. This is great for moving between work and home machines.

ai-memory is designed to work with any AI coding CLI or chat tool. If your AI can read and write text files, it can work with ai-memory.

Do not worry. ai-memory has an autosave feature. It automatically saves your session every few minutes. You can adjust this interval in the settings.
